* TI - IVA (Imaging and Video Accelerator) subsystem

The IVA contain various audio, video or imaging HW accelerator
depending of the version.

Required properties:
- compatible : Should be:
  - "ti,ivahd" for OMAP4
  - "ti,iva2.2" for OMAP3
  - "ti,iva2.1" for OMAP2430
  - "ti,iva1" for OMAP2420
- ti,hwmods: "iva"

Optional properties ("ti,ivahd", for a remote processor's resource manager):
- resets: the sequencer resets, ICONT1 and ICONT2
- reset-names: "seq0", "seq1"
- clocks: the SL2 interface clock
- clock-names: "sl2if"

Examples:

iva {
    compatible = "ti,ivahd", "ti,iva";
    ti,hwmods = "iva";
};
